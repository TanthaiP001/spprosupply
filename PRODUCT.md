# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Shoppers** — Thai consumers browsing and purchasing health and wellness products online. They arrive from search, referrals, or repeat visits; they need to trust product quality, understand pricing and payment, and complete checkout without friction on mobile or desktop.

**Admin staff** — Internal operators managing catalog, orders, users, banners, and categories. They work in focused sessions, often switching between data-heavy screens (orders, products) and quick edits (banners, categories). Speed and clarity matter more than marketing polish.

## Product Purpose

SP Pro Supply (เอส พี โปร ซัพพลาย) is an online store for creative health and wellness products. The storefront helps customers discover products, build trust, and purchase confidently. The admin area supports day-to-day operations: inventory, orders, content, and analytics. Success means shoppers feel the brand is credible and caring, and admins can run the business without fighting the interface.

## Brand Personality

Warm, natural, approachable — wellness and care. The voice is friendly and reassuring, not clinical or corporate. Health expertise shows through clear product information and thoughtful layout, not through cold authority or hype. Green is the anchor (growth, nature, health) but the feel should be human, not generic "eco-shop" template.

## Anti-references

- AI-default aesthetics: cream/sand body backgrounds, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, numbered section eyebrows on every block, hero-metric SaaS templates
- Generic Thai marketplace clones (Shopee/Lazada visual language applied to a specialty health retailer)
- Cold, gray admin dashboards with no connection to the storefront brand
- Over-designed marketing pages that slow down shopping tasks

## Design Principles

1. **Trust before flash** — Credibility (clear pricing, payment info, product detail, Thai typography) comes before decorative motion or layout experiments.
2. **Warm utility** — Every screen should feel caring and natural while staying efficient; warmth comes from tone, spacing, and imagery, not from visual noise.
3. **One brand, two modes** — Storefront and admin share identity (color, type, voice) but admin prioritizes scanability and task speed over merchandising drama.
4. **Show the product** — Photography, names, and benefits lead; UI chrome stays quiet so health products remain the hero.
5. **Mobile-first Thai** — Thai copy and Noto Sans Thai readability on small screens are non-negotiable; English labels stay secondary where both appear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG certification target. Baseline expectations: readable contrast on body text, keyboard-accessible controls, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. Prioritize legible Thai type at common mobile sizes over decorative effects.
